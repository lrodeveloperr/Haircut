# Style matrix

Status: built 6 November 2026 on the owner's direction. What a customer asks for depends on three things: the hair he
walks in with, his hair type, and who he is (age and hair loss). Every style is made with the clipper's guards 0-4.

| Walks in with | Most often asks for | Rarely asks for |
|---|---|---|
| Full, even hair | Taper, side part, fade, crew, undercut | Buzz |
| Longer top, shaped sides | Taper, side part, fade, undercut | Buzz |
| Grown-out fade | The fade or skin fade again, temple fade | Side part, buzz |
| Medium, even hair | Crew, Caesar, buzz, fade | |
| Short, even hair | Buzz, skin fade | Anything longer than he has |

| Hair type | Leans toward |
|---|---|
| Straight (1) | Side part, crew, undercut, taper, Caesar |
| Wavy (2) | Taper, fade, undercut, Caesar |
| Curly (3) | Fade, taper, skin fade, temple fade |
| Coily (4) | Skin and temple fades, burst fades, buzz, Caesar |

Age and hair loss then apply: older men lean to tapers and trims, young men to fades, and heavy hair loss goes to buzz
cuts, a full shave or a short fade. A customer only asks for a style his hair can really make.

Styles added for this: a full shave (guard 0 all over) and the Caesar.

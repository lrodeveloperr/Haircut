## 5. Ready-to-copy English listing

### Title

Barber Simulator: ASMR Haircut

### Short description

Cut fades in a 3D barber shop with clipper sounds and freehand control.

### Full description

Cut hair your way in Barber Simulator: ASMR Haircut, a 3D barber shop game built around freehand cutting and satisfying clipper sounds.

SHAPE YOUR OWN HAIRCUTS
Move the clippers through the hair, change guard lengths and blend short and long sections into a fade. Follow the shape of each head and create the cut you want.

SEE EVERY ANGLE
Rotate the customer through a full 360 degrees to reach the sides, crown and back. Check your work from different angles before the final reveal.

ENJOY THE SOUND OF EACH CUT
Hear the clipper buzz and cutting effects as you work through the hair. The ASMR-inspired sound and falling hair keep the focus on the cutting.

MEET DIFFERENT CUSTOMERS
Work with different hair textures, natural colors, face shapes and ages. Each new customer gives you a fresh look to shape.

CUSTOMIZE YOUR BARBER SETUP
Choose clipper designs and barber capes from the wardrobe.

Daily clipper designs and capes are optional unlocks. Finish two rewarded videos to unlock each daily item.

Finish the haircut, watch the reveal and move on to your next customer. A hands-on hair cutting simulator for anyone who enjoys fades, close-up detail and creative barber games.

## 7. Conversion assets

Three initial screenshot concepts, using authentic current gameplay:

1. **Your cut. Your way.** Show the clipper making a deliberate freehand fade on the actual 3D customer. The haircut is the hero.
2. **Every angle. Every detail.** Show the same customer at distinct rotation angles, making the 360-degree interaction clear.
3. **Fresh customers. New looks.** Show actual varied customers. After final Android size verification, test replacing this caption with a factual small-download message.

ASMR is communicated through the title, a short sound-on caption and an authentic gameplay preview with the original clipper/cutting audio. Use the real game, not a mock haircut that the player cannot reproduce. Do not claim no ads or copy another game's name or screenshots.

Record the actual Play device-download size after the AAB is processed. Only then use a measured size statement or a substantiated lightweight message. The old web ZIP size is not the Android download size.
